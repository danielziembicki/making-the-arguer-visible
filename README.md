# making-the-arguer-visible
Data and supplementary materials for the article “Making the Arguer Visible: Reconstruction Practices and the Representation of Speaker Intent”.

**Daniel Ziembicki, Iza Duraj-Nowosielska, Weronika Garlikowska, Zofia Ponulak  
“Making the Arguer Visible: Reconstruction Practices and the Representation of Speaker Intent”**,  
accepted for publication in *Argumentation*.

## Dataset

The dataset was created to investigate reconstruction and interpretative practices in argument annotation, with particular attention to implicit argumentative content and the representation of speaker commitments.

The source material consists of a transcript of a Polish public debate on abortion broadcast by Kanał Zero in 2024.

The dataset contains:

- 1,632 pre-defined sentence-level segments;
- annotations produced independently by 9 annotators;
- 1,800 Premise annotations;
- 965 Conclusion annotations;
- a GOLD version containing 119 Premises and 81 Conclusions.

## Annotation

The annotation scheme distinguishes two argumentative unit types:

- **Premise**
- **Conclusion**

and one relation:

- **inference**

Each annotation is accompanied by a verbalisation of the argumentative content identified by the annotator.

## Repository contents

This repository contains the dataset, annotation guidelines, and supplementary materials used in the study.
